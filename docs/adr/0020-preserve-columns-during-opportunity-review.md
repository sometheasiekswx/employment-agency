# Preserve columns during opportunity review

On 15 November 2026, the owner chose stable company columns while working through opportunities. Saving, removing, undoing and background updates preserve the positions and relative order of unaffected companies and roles. A vacated space closes only within its current column; when a company empties, the next company already below it moves up. An exhausted column may remain empty. This deliberately trades some packing density for the ability to keep track of roles during repeated decisions.

Refresh matches, a filter/profile/view change, or a width change that changes the column count may arrange the board again. New companies append during the current review instead of displacing existing ones. Brief, interruptible movement explains local changes, respects reduced motion, and preserves existing Undo and keyboard behavior. The [specification](../../tasks/stable-opportunity-board/spec.md) records acceptance and verification details. This decision does not change application choices, company allowances or submission authorization.
